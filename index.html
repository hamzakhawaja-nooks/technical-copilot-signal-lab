MEASUREMENT CONTRACT

What counts—and what doesn’t

01

Question moment

One distinct external technical, nuanced product, troubleshooting, integration, dialer, caller, or phone-number question. A call can contain multiple moments.

02

Punted moment

The Nooks employee indicates the answer will come later: checking, investigating, asking someone, following up, or circling back.

03

Strict exclusions

Scheduling, commercial next steps, collateral, confident answers, or “I don’t know” without a later-answer commitment do not count.

04

Attribution

Each moment belongs only to the employee who deferred. The same moment is not duplicated when multiple selected employees join the call.

Recommended success read: A falling punt percentage indicates improvement only when transcript coverage and the distribution of question categories remain stable.

DATA QUALITY

Why this result is directional

Question-level results require both confidently answered and deferred company-knowledge questions. Nooks2 was used to identify transcript-bearing records and analyze substantive external conversations; the displayed rates remain directional where short, non-conversational, missing, or truncated transcripts were not reviewable.

  • ✓ External-only customer-call filter applied
  • ✓ Ten specified employee participants included
  • ✓ Strict later-answer rule applied to the numerator
  • ○ Missing and low-signal transcripts remain a coverage limitation
